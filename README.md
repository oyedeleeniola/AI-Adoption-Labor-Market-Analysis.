# AI Adoption & Technical Labor Market Analysis

## Project Overview
This Power BI project explores the relationship between AI adoption and technical job postings, AI-related hiring, salaries, and AI skills requirements.

It also examines differences across regions, age groups, and company sizes.

## Research Question
How does AI adoption within companies relate to changes in technical job postings and average technical salaries, and how does this relationship vary by region, age group, and company size?

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX

## Dataset
The project uses a synthetic dataset covering:
- 12 companies
- 12 months
- 6 AI tools
- 5 age groups

## Analysis
- AI adoption and technical job postings
- AI adoption and AI-related job postings
- Technical salaries
- AI skills requirements
- Regional comparisons
- Age-group comparisons
- Company-size comparisons
- Monthly trends

## Limitations
The dataset is synthetic and does not represent verified real-world labor-market outcomes.

Correlation does not prove causation. Salary comparisons across countries also require currency normalization.
